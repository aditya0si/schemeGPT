## Eligibility

The applicant should be a citizen of India.The
applicant should be a domicile / permanent resident of the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir.The applicant should be a child less
than 18 years of age. In exceptional cases, it can be extended up to the age of 21 years.The applicant should be an Orphan or
Destitute.The applicant's main bread earner should have either been killed or permanently incapacitated in communal, caste, ethnic, or
terrorist violence. ​The applicant's family should be 'Below Poverty Line' (BPL).The applicant should not be receiving any assistance on a
regular basis from any other source.NOTE: The one-time financial assistance provided by the State Government / Union Territory
Administration for such a victim is not considered 'another source of assistance' for this purpose.
