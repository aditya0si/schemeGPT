## Details

The sub-scheme “Day Care - cum - Recreation Centre and
Physiotherapy Unit" under the umbrella scheme "Grant-in-Aid to Pondicherry Society for the Care of the Aged (PONCARE)" was
launched by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Puducherry. Through this scheme, the senior citizens are provided free
physiotherapy given by the physiotherapist, as advised by the doctors.
