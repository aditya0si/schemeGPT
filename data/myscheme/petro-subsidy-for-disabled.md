## Details

The Petrol Subsidy To Persons With Disability scheme under the
Department Social Welfare, Women & Child Development of Chandigarh will handicapped persons using motorized vehicles entitled to
a 
50% subsidy on actual expenditure on the purchase of petrol/diesel subject to fulfillment of other conditions laid down under the
scheme. The physically handicapped persons having income up to ₹1.50 lacs from all sources are eligible for subsidy. The physically
handicapped persons who are already in receipt of the conveyance allowance either from any voluntary or State source shall not be
eligible for a grant of subsidy on the purchase of petrol/diesel. Petrol/Diesel Subsidy @50% of actual expenditure on purchase of
PetrolDieselp to a maximum of 20 Ltrs. Per month.
