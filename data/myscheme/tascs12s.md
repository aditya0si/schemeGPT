## Benefits

Under the scheme, students in the 12th standard belonging to the Scheduled Caste
category will receive a tablet by paying a token fee of ₹1,000/-.

## Eligibility

The student should belong to the Scheduled Caste of
Gujarat.The student should be studying in 12th Standard.There is no income limit.Note: The students should have received a tablet from
the education department by paying a token fee of ₹1,000/
