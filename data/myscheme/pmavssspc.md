Professional courses are divided into A, B, and C
categories and accordingly, maintenance allowance is given to the students through the concerned Principal of the college.For 4 to 5
years course (Medical, Engineering, Agriculture, etc.) Rs. 700/- per month, for 2 to 3 years course (Engineering Dip., M.B.A, M.S.W,
etc.) Rs. 500/- and for 2 years and lesser duration courses (B.Ed., D.Ed.) Rs. 500/- per month for 10 months as maintenance allowance in
addition to other Government Scholarships.
