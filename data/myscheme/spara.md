## Benefits

In this scheme every year
₹51000/- is awarded with the shawl and certificate to the farmer who gains the first position in each category and also first five farmers
from each category are appreciated with the shawl & certificate.In addition to this, farmers as in the form of encouragement are
appreciated by giving shawls & certificates for the special work done in the field of beekeeping, poultry farming, fisheries,
etc.

## Eligibility

The farmer should be a permanent resident of Gujarat State.The farmers either Male or Female who possesses Agriculture
land in Gujarat are eligible to apply for this scheme.
