## Details

Launched in the year 2013-14, "Biju Yuva
Sashaktikaran Yojana" is a scheme by the Department of Higher Education, Govt. of Odisha. In this scheme, ₹ 30,000/- per student is transferred directly to the bank accounts of 15,000 meritorious +2 students through
Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) for the purchase of laptop. The allocation earmarked to SC & ST students in a particular stream is bifurcated between SC and ST as per the ratio 16.25:22.5, which is also the reservation
ratio in employment in the State. The scheme is applicable to students from the CHSE Board, and Upshastri Students passing from Shree Jagannath Sanskrit University, Puri.
