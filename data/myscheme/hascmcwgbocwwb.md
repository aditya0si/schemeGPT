## Benefits

Under the scheme, children of migrant construction workers should be provided free accommodation in primary
school.

## Eligibility

The children of registered construction workers who are studying in the government primary and secondary schools of
the district can avail the benefits under the scheme.The construction workers should be registered with the Gujarat Building and Other
Construction Worker’s Welfare Board.
