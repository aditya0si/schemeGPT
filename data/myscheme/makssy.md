## Details

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme "Mukhyamantri Asangathit
Karmakaar Saayakal Sahaayata Yojana" for unorganized worker on 28 April 2012. Under this scheme, One cycle will be provided free
of cost to each women beneficiary. The main objective of the scheme to empower the unorganized female worker through provide them
free cycle.

## Benefits

A free of cost cycle will be provided.
