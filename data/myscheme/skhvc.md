## Details

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme "Safai Karmakaar Ke Putr/Putri Hetu
Vishesh Coaching Yojana" for unorganized worker on 11 March 2015. The main objective of the scheme to provide financial assistance
for free coaching.

## Benefits

The fee for special coaching for admission in engineering, medical, CA and MBA or other professional
education to the son/daughter (two children) studying in class 10th and above will be paid by the board.

## Eligibility
