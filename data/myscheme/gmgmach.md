## Details

The scheme “Grant of Mahatma Gandhi Memorial Award for Clean Houses” was
introduced by the Adi Dravidar Welfare Department, Government of Puducherry. The objective of the scheme is to motivate the
Scheduled Caste people to keep their houses neat and tidy. Incentives in the form of household articles worth ₹500/- for each selected
beneficiary is being given.
