## Details

The Simple Marriage scheme is a government initiative by the Social Welfare Department of Karnataka that offers
incentives to couples belonging to the SC community who opt for a simple marriage ceremony. The scheme aims to promote the concept
of simple marriages and provide financial assistance to eligible couples. Under this scheme, the Social Welfare Department of
Karnataka will provide incentives to Simple Married Couples belonging to the SC Community who have submitted their applications
within 1 year of marriage. Only couples who got married on or after 11-08-2015 are eligible for the incentive, which is set at Rs.
50,000.00. To be eligible for the scheme, the applicant must be a resident of Karnataka state, belong to the SC community, and have a
family income from all sources that does not exceed Rs. 2 lakhs per annum. It is important to note that the scheme is applicable within 1
year of marriage
