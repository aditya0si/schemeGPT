## Eligibility

The applicants must be permanent residents of Assam state.The applicants must avail the housing loan from
any Scheduled Commercial Bank, Regional Rural Banks, Assam Cooperative Apex bank within the state.The total family income of the applicant (from all sources) must not exceed ₹ 20,00,000.The housing loan must
be of more than ₹ 5,00,000 and sanctioned by bank on or after 1st April 2019.The loan accounts must not be under NPA (Non Performing Assets) status.This must be the first home by the composite
family.ExclusionsThose who already benefited under the Apon Ghar scheme are not eligible.
