## Benefits

Under the scheme, financial assistance up to 75% of the purchase cost of powered chaff cutter or ₹18,000/-, whichever
is less, is given to beneficiaries of Scheduled Caste category. 
Note: The amount of assistance will be deposited in bank account of the
beneficiary.

## Eligibility

The beneficiary should be a permanent resident of Gujarat.The beneficiary should belong to the Scheduled Caste
category.The beneficiary should have a minimum of five (5) animals of his/her own.
