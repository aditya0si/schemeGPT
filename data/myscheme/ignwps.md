## Details

Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS) is implemented by Ministry of
Rural Development, Government of India. It is a non-contributory pension scheme to provide social security to widows of poor family
(BPL) 
of the society.

## Benefits

A pension of Rs.300/- per month is provided to Widows between 40 years and 79 years. For persons who
are 80 years and above the pension is Rs.500/- per month.
