## Details

The “Farmers Training Institute Scheme” is a State Plan Scheme, which is
implemented by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya. The objective of the scheme is to
provide training to farmers at the village level on updated and innovative agriculture technology. The Training Institute facilitates
exposure visits for farmers to agriculturally advanced states. The Scheme also facilitates exposure visits for farmers within and outside
the State.

## Benefits

Pattern of Assistance:Farmers are imparted specialised training at the center including in the farmers' field.Exposure
programmes and field tours are also conducted for farmers' groups.Type of Assistance and Entitlement (If any):Assist in Training and
capacity building and sponsoring Progressive/Award winning farmers for Exposure trips within/outside the State.
