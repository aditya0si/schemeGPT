## Eligibility

The beneficiary should be working in the Establishment, registered under the Punjab
Labour Welfare Fund Act, 1965 and his contribution towards the labour welfare fund should regularly been received by the Board.In
case of the death of a registered male construction worker, his dependent widow should apply for this scheme within one year from the
date of death.Registered construction workers with full or 50% disability due to an accident are eligible to apply under the scheme.The
beneficiary has not availed any financial assistance from any other Government body.
