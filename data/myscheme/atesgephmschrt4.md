## Benefits

The Scheduled Castes farmers will be
eligible for assistance up to 75% of the cost of the Tools/Equipment or ₹3,750/-, whichever is less.Farmer groups/cooperative societies
will be eligible for assistance of 90% of the cost of equipment or up to ₹7,50,000/-, whichever is less, for the installation of Post-
Harvest Management (PHM) Equipment.
