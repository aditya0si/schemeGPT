## Benefits

Under this Program, a fellowship amount of ₹55,000/- and HRA (as per norms) per month will be
given to the eligible candidates.The selected fellow will also be eligible for a Research Grant of ₹2.00 Lakhs per year, a Field
Demonstration, etc. of ₹1.00 Lakh (for project tenure), and the cost of small equipment (up to a maximum of ₹2.00 Lakhs) along with
Institutional overhead charges as per DST norms.
