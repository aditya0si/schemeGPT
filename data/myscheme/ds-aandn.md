## Details

The “Dulari Scheme” was launched by the Health Department, Union Territory of Andaman and
Nicobar Islands on 26th January, 2013 to give incentives to newborn baby girls and girl children on passing VIII, X & XII standards. As
per the present scheme, the girl child born on or after 26.01.2013 will be eligible to get the benefit under the scheme for birth incentives
and the girl children who are below 13 years of age not registered under the Planned family scheme (old scheme) will also get the benefit
of this scheme.
