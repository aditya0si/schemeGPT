## Benefits

1. The financial assistance is given to farmers for the purchase of tractors as below: 
a) Upto 40
PTO HP Model Tractors: 25% of expense or ₹45,000/-, whichever is less. 
b) Above 40 PTO HP and up to 60 PTO HP Model Tractors:
25% of expense or ₹60,000/-, whichever is less.

## Eligibility

The beneficiary should be a permanent resident of Gujarat State.Any farmer
of Gujarat state is eligible under this scheme.The beneficiary farmer has to purchase the empaneled tractor model of the manufacturer
included in the empanelment prepared for the purpose of prize discovery announced by the agriculture department from time to time for
tractors from their authorized dealer.
