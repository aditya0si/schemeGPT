## Benefits

The Distinguished Visiting Professor
will be reimbursed air-fare in “Y-Class” or actual fares if performed by Rail or approved INAE rate if travel is performed by Road.
The Distinguished Visiting Professoris entitled to an honorarium of ₹ 10,000/- per lecture day.For on-line sessions of DVP Programs,
the Experts will be paid only Honorariums of ₹ 10,000/- per day without travelling/lodging expenses based on mutually decided
timings between concerned DVPs in association with the concerned Institutes.

## Eligibility

The Industry Experts should have a minimum
graduate degree in engineering or an equivalent qualification.The Age of a Distinguished Visiting Professor shall be in the range of 40 to
75 years.The applicant must have minimum Ten (10) years experience in Industry; and the individual must have distinguished work
experience, as evidenced by development of products, technology, processes, patents, publications, awards etc.All retired INAE Fellows
also can apply under this Scheme.Note: For exceptional cases the Selection Committee may relax age limits.ExclusionsThe DVP on
completion of a tenure is not eligible to re-apply.
