## Eligibility

The applicant should be a permanent resident of Kerala.The applicant should be a
student.The applicant should be studying in Government / Aided Institution in Kerala.The financial assistance will be given to the
students who are studying in Class 9th to Post-Graduation.The applicant who has 40% or more disability is eligible to apply under the
scheme.The applicant’s annual family income is not applicable under this scheme.Note: The preference will be given to the students
of Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.
