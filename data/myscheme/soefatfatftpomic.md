## Details

"Scheme of Extending Financial Assistance to Folk Artists, Troupes for the Purchase of Musical
Instruments, Costumes" was launched by the Art and Culture Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme provides a sum of
₹2,000/- to individual folk artists & ₹6,000/- for cultural troupes, as a grant for the purchase of Musical Instruments and
Costumes.

## Benefits

For Folk Artists, financial Assistance of ₹2,000/-.For Troupes, financial Assistance of ₹6,000/-.

## Eligibility

The
applicant should be a Folk Artist/Troupe.The applicant should be continuously performing in the programs.The applicant should have
performed in the programs for more than three years.
