## Details

"Scheme of Awarding Porkizhi to the Indigent Kalaimamani Awardee
Artists" was launched by the Art and Culture Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The artists who have already received the
Kalaimamani Award and who are presently in indigent circumstances are awarded financial assistance of ₹15,000/-. The scheme is
implemented by the Tamil Nadu Eyal Isai Nataka Mandram Ponni.

## Benefits

Financial Assistance of ₹15,000/-.
