## Details

The "Cash Benefit Scheme" is a Welfare Scheme by the Puducherry Building
and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, Labour Department, Union Territory of Puducherry. The objective of this scheme is to
provide educational financial assistance to the wards of construction workers who have secured the top three ranks at the State Level and
Regional Level in the Higher Secondary course or its equivalent examinations. The amount is credited through Direct Benefit Transfer
(DBT) into the bank account of the nominee/ dependent. The applications are accepted offline.
