## Details

Launched on 1st April 2020, “Jai Johar (Old Age Pension)” is a Social Security Scheme by the Tribal
Development Department, Government of West Bengal. The scheme is aimed at the Scheduled Tribe (ST) residents of West Bengal of
the age of 60 years and above who do not receive any other pension from any other authority. Over 3,00,000 beneficiaries have been
brought under the fold of the scheme. The amount of monthly pension of ₹ 1,000/- is transferred directly to the individual bank account
of the beneficiary.

## Benefits

A monthly pension of ₹1,000/- is transferred directly to the individual bank account of the
beneficiary.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be a permanent resident of West Bengal.The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Tribe
(ST) category.The applicant should have attained the age of 60 years or more.The applicant should not be a beneficiary under any other
Social Security pension scheme.The applicant should not be a recipient of Government pension or pension from any other
organization.
