## Details

The Government of Himachal Pradesh, Department of Social Justice & Empowerment has launched Old Age Pension
Scheme with the aim to provide social security to destitute elderly people of the state. In this scheme, a financial assistance of ₹ 850/-
per month to pensioners aged between 60 years to 69 years and ₹ 1500/- per month to pensioners aged 70 years and above is
provided.

## Benefits

₹ 850/- per month to pensioners aged between 60 years to 69 years.₹ 1500/- per month to pensioners aged 70 years
and above.

## Eligibility

The applicants must be a resident of Himachal Pradesh state.Such elderly persons whose age is 60 years or more
and who do not have proper means of care/upbringing and who have minor children and whose family annual income from all sources
does not exceed ₹ 35,000/- Or;Such elderly persons whose annual family income from all sources does not exceed ₹ 35000/- are
eligible for the scheme.
