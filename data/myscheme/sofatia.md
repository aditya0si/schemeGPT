## Details

The "Scheme of Financial Assistance to Indigent Artists" was launched by the Art and Culture Department,
Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance of ₹3,000/- per month to artists who are in indigent
circumstances and their dependents. The Indigent artists who have rendered services in the field of Art and Culture during their lifetime
ar eligible for this scheme.

## Benefits

Financial Assistance of ₹3,000/- per month.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be an artist.The
applicant should be in indigent circumstances.The age of the applicant should be 58 years or above.The applicant should have
professionally served for the enrichment of one or more art disciplines such as music, dance, drama, film folk arts, etc.The annual
income of the applicant or their legal spouse should not exceed ₹12,000/-.
