## Details

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme "Nai Hetu
Avashyak Upkaran Sahayata Yojana" for unorganized worker on 
27 January 2018.. The main objective of the scheme to empower
unorganized worker by providing required equipment for their work.

## Benefits

Registered barbers will be provided barber kit including
scissors, straight razor, comb, hair cutting cape, water spray bottle and shaving brush.

## Eligibility

Applicant must be registered
Unorganized worker of State Social Security Board. Applicant age must be between 18 to 60 years. Applicant must not receiving any
other parallel scheme of the state government
