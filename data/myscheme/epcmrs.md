## Benefits

Reimbursement of 80% or ₹ 20,000/-
(excluding GST and other applicable taxes), whichever is less, in a financial year to SC/ST MSEs on annual membership subscription
fee / one time subscription charges / entry fee charged by various Export Promotion Councils (EPC).

## Eligibility

Applicant should
belong to Scheduled Caste(SC)/Scheduled Tribe (ST) category. He/She should represent any Micro and Small Enterprises
(MSEs).
