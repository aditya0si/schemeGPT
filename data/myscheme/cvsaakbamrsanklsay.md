## Eligibility

The applicant should be a native of Madhya
Pradesh.The applicant should have a Certificate of 40 percent or more disability.The applicant's name should be mentioned on the
Samagra portal and verified on the Sparsh portal The applicant should have an age certificate for being above six years of
age.
