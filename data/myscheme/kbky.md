## Benefits

Farmer training within the state : ₹1,000/- per farmer per month (maximum 7 days)Farmer
training outside the state : ₹1,500/- per farmer per day.Exposure visit outside the state to observe new technology (new component) :
₹1,500/- per farmer per day.A total of ₹12,000/- for 20 trainees in one-day farmer training and tour, i.e. maximum expenditure per
farmer is ₹500/- and honorarium to the host farmer is ₹100/-, thus the total maximum expenditure is Rs 600/- per
farmer.

## Eligibility

This scheme will apply to all sections.Farmers of all classes who cultivate crops.
