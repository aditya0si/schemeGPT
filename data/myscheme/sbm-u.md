## Details

A sanitation scheme for the construction of Individual Household Latrines (IHHL) for urban households. The Mission has
the following components:Household toilets, including conversion of insanitary latrines into pour-flush latrines.Community toiletsPublic
toiletsSolid waste managementIEC & Public AwarenessCapacity building and Administrative & Office Expenses (A&OE)

Union Territories) is provided for Toilet Construction

## Eligibility

Any household that does not have access to an individual
household toilet ( 80% of urban households engaging in open defecation) or has an insanitary toilet.
