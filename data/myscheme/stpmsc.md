## Eligibility

The student should belong to the Scheduled Tribe category.The student who is studying as a regular student in
classes 6 to 8 in schools run by the Central Government, State Government, Municipalities and schools recognized by the Department of
Education.Students whose parents/guardians are not income taxpayers when they are not alive.The student who is not getting any other
type of scholarship or allowance for study from central, state / public sources.The student who has not failed in the lower class in the last
session. If he fails in any year's examination, his scholarship will be stopped, but if he fails in the same class in the nextIf he passes the
examination, he will be eligible for the scholarship in the new class from the next session. For this, the student will have to apply for a
new scholarship again.Students who are not living in hostels are run or aided by the Department of Social Justice and
Empowerment.
