## Benefits

Token Remunerationa) Interns will receive a stipend of Rs. 15,000 per month, payable at Rs.
15,000 for each four weeks.b) Interns admitted under relaxed qualification criteria outlined in paragraph 4(b) will not receive any
stipend.c) The stipend will be disbursed upon completion of the internship, submission of an accepted report by the relevant reporting
authority, and endorsement by the Secretary/Director (Operations) at Headquarters.Additionally, interns will be reimbursed for
economy-class airfare and Taxi Travel for their visits to the land ports. Boarding and lodging arrangements will be provided at the land
port visited by the interns.Certificate of InternshipUpon completion of the internship and submission of the report, certificates will be
issued to interns by Headquarters, provided the report is countersigned and accepted by the competent authority.
