criteria

total limit of ₹30,000/- consisting of ₹15,000/- by Standard 11th and ₹15,000/- for Standard 12th for private tuition to students of

Digital Gujarat Portal at https://www.digitalgujarat.gov.in/.Can students from other states apply for this scheme?No, this scheme is
