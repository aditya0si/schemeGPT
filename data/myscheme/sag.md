## Details

A support scheme by Ministry of Women & Child Development enabling self-development
and empowerment of out-of-school adolescent girls, and improving their nutritional, health & hygiene 
status. The scheme aims at
supporting out of school AGs to successfully transition back to formal schooling or bridge learning / skill training. Upgrade their home-
based skills and life skills. Provide information/guidance about existing public services such as Primary Health Centers, Rural
Hospitals/CHCs, Post Office, Bank, Police Station, etc. Scheme for Adolescent Girls - SAG is implemented using the platform of
Anganwadi Services of Umbrella ICDS Scheme through Anganwadi Centers (AWCs).

## Benefits

Nutrition Component:Take Home Ration
or Hot Cooked Meal for out of school girls - Nutrition Provision ₹ 9.50 per day (600 calories; 18”20 gram of protein and
recommended daily intake of micronutrients per day for 300 days in a year.). The financial norms will be ₹ 9.5/- per beneficiary per day
for 300 days in a year. This would be inclusive of the cost of micronutrient fortification. Non Nutrition Component:IFA
supplementation.Health check”up and Referral services.Nutrition & Health Education (NHE).Counseling / Guidance on family welfare,
ARSH, child care practices.Life Skill Education and accessing public services.

## Eligibility

The applicant must be a FEMALE.The age of
the applicant must be between 11 and 14 years.ExclusionsNA

## Application Process

OfflineThe out-of-school adolescent girls (11 - 14
years age) are identified by the State / UT through a Base Line Survey, a one-time activity carried out by the Anganwadi Workers
(AWW) who should go from house to house to meet every family in the area covered by her AWC, and gather information about the
details of such girls. Such girls are provided the benefits through the Anganwadi Centre (AWC).
