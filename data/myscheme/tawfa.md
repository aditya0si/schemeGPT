## Eligibility

1. The applicant must be and artist in the field of art and culture of the State.2.
The applicant must be above the age of 20 years (in case of applicant being the artist).3. The applicant should be in distress.4. The
applicant should have a made a mark or made some achievement in the field of art and culture.5. The applicant should be able to provide
artistic credentials supporting his/her achievement.
