## Details

The Rajasthan
Prasuti Sahayata Yojana offers a lifeline to pregnant women from registered construction worker families in the state. Launched by the
government, the scheme provides crucial financial support at a critical juncture, aiming to improve the well-being of both mother and
child. This assistance empowers women to prioritize their health throughout pregnancy and ensure proper care for their
newborns.

## Benefits

The birth of a daughter: ₹ 21,000/-The birth of a son : ₹ 20,000/-In case of not receiving cash benefits under Janani
Suraksha Yojana, ₹1,000/- additional assistance will be payable.
