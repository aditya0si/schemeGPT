## Eligibility

Should be studying regularly in 11th and 12th in government schools.The girl student should belong to
the Scheduled Tribe category of Rajasthan.The parent/guardian/guardian/husband of the student should not be an income tax payer.The
student must be a native of Rajasthan.The student who is studying by living in the hostel run by the government. They will not be entitled
to benefit under the said scheme.Must have Vaidya Bhamashah card and Aadhaar card.
