## Details

ObjectivesThe main objective of this scheme is to provide financial assistance in the form of unemployment allowance to
the educated youth of the state who are searching for employment. This scheme helps those who are educated and talented but cannot
generate enough income to apply for government jobs or other jobs due to the financial crisis.

## Benefits

The government will
provide financial assistance of Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 1,500 till the time youths get desired job.Private and government jobs are available on
one portal.Online application facility.Facility to search jobs by category, location, department, and salary.
