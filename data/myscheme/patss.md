## Details

The Himachal
Pradesh Council for Science, Technology & Environment (HIMCOSTE), has a scheme for providing partial travel assistance to
deserving bright young Scientists and Research Scholars working in an academic or research institution located in Himachal Pradesh for
participating in a National/ and International Conference/ Seminar/ Symposium/ Workshop held within India or abroad. The scheme
does not provide assistance towards lodging, boarding, maintenance, registration fees, taxi fare and other costs

## Benefits

Partial
Coverage: The scheme provides support for a portion of the total travel expenses incurred by the beneficiaries.Financial Assistance: For
international travel, the scheme offers support up to a maximum of Rs. 60,000/- or 60% of the total travel expenses, whichever is lower.
This financial assistance helps offset a significant portion of the travel costs for international trips.In-Country Travel Support: For travel
within the country, the scheme provides a maximum support of Rs. 10,000/- or 60% of the total travel expenses, whichever is lower. This
assistance helps beneficiaries cover a portion of their travel costs within the country.Flexibility: The scheme allows beneficiaries to
utilize the financial support as per their travel requirements, be it international or in-country travel.State Council Support: The State
Council is responsible for providing the specified travel support under this scheme, ensuring that beneficiaries receive the designated
financial assistance.

## FAQs

Who is eligible to apply for the PATSS scheme?Scientists and Research Scholars working in
an academic or research institution located in Himachal Pradesh, who have passed at least M.Sc./B.E./B.Tech/MBBS or equivalent
degree from a recognized University/Institute, are eligible to apply.What is the maximum age limit to be considered for travel assistance?
The applicant's age should not be above 40 years on the date of commencement of the Conference/Seminar/Symposium/Workshop.Is it
mandatory for the paper to be accepted for presentation?Yes, the applicant's paper should have been accepted for presentation. A copy of
the full paper, along with an abstract, schedule of the conference, and acceptance letter from the organizer, must be appended with the
application.Can scientists and research scholars who have previously received travel support under PATSS apply again?No, scientists
and research scholars who have received travel support under PATSS in the previous three years are not eligible for consideration.Are
applications to attend annual meetings of professional societies considered under this scheme?No, applications to attend annual meetings
of professional societies will not be considered. The Conference/Seminar/Symposium/Workshop should be of an international character
and repute.Is publication in a refereed journal necessary to be considered for travel assistance?Yes, scientists and research scholars who
have not published at least one paper in a refereed journal would not be considered.What is the procedure for forwarding the application?
The application, along with all necessary documents, should be forwarded through the proper channel from the Head of the
Institution.What is the deadline for submitting the application?Applications complete in all respects should reach the office mandatorily
one month before the event.What is the requirement for the research paper presented at the conference?The scientist/research scholar
sanctioned travel grant under the scheme is required to provide one reprint of the research paper presented at the conference after its
publication.
