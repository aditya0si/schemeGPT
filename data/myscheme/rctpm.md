## Details

The scheme “Regional Centre for Training
and Production of Mushroom” was launched by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya to
train farmers in the method of mushroom cultivation and to supply quality spawn and pasteurised compost to farmers.

## Eligibility

The applicant/farmer should be a permanent resident of Meghalaya.The
applicant should be a bonafide farmer engaged in agriculture, possessing own or leased land of at least 0.2 (hectares) or more.Note: The
applicant farmer can avail of the benefits of the scheme on a first come first serve basis.
