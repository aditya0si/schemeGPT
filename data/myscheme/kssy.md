## Details

The "Kalinga Sikhya Sathi Yojana
(KSSY)" is an Educational Loan Scheme by the Department of Higher Education, Govt. of Odisha. The scheme was launched by the
Hon’ble CM of Odisha, Shri Naveen Patnaik on 27th June 2016. The objective of the scheme is to bring down the financial burden
on the parents of the students who want to move forward with higher education. A student can get a maximum of ₹ 10,00,000 as an
educational loan under this scheme. The applicants will have to pay only 1% interest on the loan amount. The balance amount of the
interest will be borne by the Government of Odisha, Higher Education Department. All the courses like medicine, engineering, law,
management, etc are covered under this scheme.
