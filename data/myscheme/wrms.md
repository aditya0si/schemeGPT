## Details

The Widow Re-Marriage Scheme of Himachal Pradesh is a government scheme aimed at
providing financial assistance to widows who have remarried. The primary objective of this scheme is to help in the rehabilitation of
widows after remarriage. Widow Re-Marriage Scheme launched by the Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of
Himachal Pradesh. The main purpose of the scheme is to help in the rehabilitation of widows, by encouraging men to enter into wedlock
with widows. This scheme provides financial assistance to such widows. Under this scheme financial assistance of Rs. 25, 000 is
provided out of which cash grant of Rs. 10,000 will be given to widow at the time of marriage and Rs. 15,000 will be kept in the form of
Fixed Deposit jointly at least for five years. The initiative has helped many destitute widows of the state. All widow permanent residents
of Himachal can apply to the scheme. More details and application form can be obtained at nearest District welfare office, the
Government of Himachal Pradesh.
