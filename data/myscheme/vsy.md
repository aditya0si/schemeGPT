## Eligibility

The applicant should be a domicile in the state of Jharkhand.The applicant should be a Worker registered with the
'Jharkhand Building and Other Construction Worker Welfare Board (JBOCWW Board)'.The applicant should be between 18-60 years of
age.The applicant should be engaged in construction work such as masons, carpenters, electricians, porters, painters, etc.The registered
worker should have made continuous contributions for five years. The bride should be at least 18 years of age. The groom should be at
least 21 years of age.The scheme is applicable for up to two children / female members of the family.Application
