## Eligibility

The applicant should be a citizen of India.The applicant should have resided continuously in the Union Territory of
Puducherry for at least five years.The applicant should be from Scheduled Caste.The applicant should be at least 18 years of age.The
annual income of the applicant should not be more than ₹2,00,000/-.
