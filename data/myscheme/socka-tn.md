## Details

The "Scheme of Conferring ‘Kalaimamani’ Award - Tamil Nadu" was launched by the
Art & Culture Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The awards felicitate the famous artists who are serving in a commendable
manner in the fields of Eyal, Isai, Natakam, Natyam, Cinema, Isai Natakam, Folk arts, Television & related arts given investiture gold
pendant of three sovereign ponnadai and citation.

## Benefits

The artist is provided with an Investiture Gold Pendant of three Sovereign
Ponnadai and a Citation.*If the artist expires after the announcement of the award to him/her but before the investiture function, the
award will be given to his/her dependent.
