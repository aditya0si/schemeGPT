## Benefits

Under the scheme, children of
laborers who have passed 70% or above marks in the 10th Standard will receive ₹2,500/- as an award.The children of laborers who
have passed 70% or above marks in the 12th Standard will receive ₹5,000/- as an award.

## Eligibility

Only the children (students) whose
parents are working in a factory/institute in the state of Gujarat and whose parents have been paying the Labour Welfare Fund will be
eligible for this scheme.The children of labourers who have passed 70% and above marks in the 10th or 12th Standard can avail the
benefits under the scheme.
