## Eligibility

The applicant should be a native and resident of Kerala State.Disabled persons with 40% or more disability are eligible to
apply under the scheme.The combined annual family income should not exceed ₹1,00,000/-.The Medical Board should certify that the
applicant is capable to use the assistive device. ExclusionsPersons with Disabilities who have already received assistive devices from
