## Eligibility

Beneficiary must be a resident of Uttar Pradesh.Beneficiary must belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled
Tribe(ST) category.Beneficiaries who are dependents of people in occupations traditionally considered
untouchable.Financial:Component 1: Beneficiaries' parents' annual income should not be more than ₹2,50,000/-.Component 2: No
income criteria.All the children of the applicant's parents will be eligible.Students cannot receive other scholarships alongside this one
(with some exceptions).Beneficiary's age limit is 12 to 20 years old (as of July 1st).
