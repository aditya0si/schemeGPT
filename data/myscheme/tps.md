## Details

The Transgender Pension Scheme is a welfare initiative launched by the Government of Himachal Pradesh
for the upliftment and support of transgender persons. The scheme provides a monthly pension of Rs. 650 to transgender persons who
are above 18 years of age and belong to the BPL (Below Poverty Line) category. A monthly pension of Rs. 1200 is provided to
transgender persons who are above 80 years or 70% disability. The main objective of this scheme is to provide financial assistance to
transgender persons and ensure their social security and protection.
