## Benefits

Under the scheme, the maximum amount of financial assistance will be ₹30,000/- per person.

## Eligibility

The
applicant should be a native and resident of Kerala State.The applicants must belong to Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.Either the
father or the mother should be undergoing imprisonment for more than two years.Note 01: The application must be submitted after six
months of marriage and within one year period. Note 02: A maximum of two girls from a family can get financial assistance for
marriage.Note 03: Once the marriage grant has been sanctioned, if the existing marriage is dissolved and remarried for any reason, the
grant will not be eligible.
