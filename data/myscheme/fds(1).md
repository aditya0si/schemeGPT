## Details

The scheme ‘Fruit Development Scheme’ was launched by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers’
Welfare, Government of Meghalaya to make available to the farmers good quality planting materials in convergence mode and sale at
subsidized rates.

## Benefits

Pattern of Assistance:Quality planting materials for sale at 50% subsidy and free of cost to the farmers under
convergent mode with MGNREGA.Type of Assistance and Entitlement (If any):Sl. No.EntitlementOwantum of Assistance1To promote
