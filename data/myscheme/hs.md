## Details

The housing subsidy scheme was launched by the Government of Himachal Pradesh to provide
financial assistance to individuals from marginalized sections for the construction of their own houses. Under this scheme, a subsidy of
Rs. 1,30,000/- is provided to persons from Scheduled Castes, Other Backward Classes, Minority Communities, Single Women, or
handicapped for the construction of their houses. This scheme aims to improve the living conditions and overall quality of life of
individuals from these sections.
