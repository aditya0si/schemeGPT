## Benefits

1. A grant of ₹8000/- is given to
the registered construction workers to buy new and suitable tool kits.2. The grant facility is available once in three years.

## Eligibility

1. The
worker should be registered under Haryana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (HBOCWWB).2. The registered
worker should have minimum one-year regular membership.
