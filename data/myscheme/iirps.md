## FAQs

What is AICTE-ISTE Induction/Refresher Program scheme? AICTE-ISTE Induction/ Refresher
Program provide financial assistance to the institutions/universites for conducting training to teaching faculties working in technical
institutions.Who manages this scheme?This scheme is managed by AICTE and ISTE.Mention one of the main benefits of the
