## Details

Mukhyamantri Kisan Kalyan Yojana
has been started by the Government of Madhya Pradesh for all the poor farmers of MP. The objective of this important scheme is to
improve the economic condition of the farmers and provide them sufficient amount for farming. Many times farmers need money to
maintain farming, for which Kisan Kalyan Yojana has been started.Chief Minister Kisan Kalyan Yojana is to fulfill the concept of
making agriculture a profitable business, to use advanced technology for agriculture, to fulfill the objective of increasing the income of
farmers and to make the farmers of the state self-reliant.
