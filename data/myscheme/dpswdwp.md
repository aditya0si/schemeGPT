## Eligibility

A woman who is a widow, divorced, separated, abandoned, deserted, or destitute woman above the age of 18 years of
lifelong.Residence of a minimum of 5 years in Delhi before the date of application. The annual income of the applicant should be less
than ₹ 1,00,000/-per annum from all sources (including rent, interest/dividends on savings & investments, earnings from the farm,
property sale proceeds, etc.) of the applicant. Applicant should have the Aadhar number The Applicant should have a single-operated
Aadhar-linked account with any bank in the NCT of Delhi only. Must not be receiving any pension/ financial assistance from Central
Govt./ State Govt./MCD or NDMC and other Governmental/local bodies or any other source for this purpose.ExclusionsNAApplication

## Documents Required

Aadhar CardSelf-attested documentary proof for the
following needs to be scanned and uploaded. Age proof Proof of Death of Husband/ Divorce decree/ Separation paper/ Divorce
proceedings/ any other document which establishes abandoned/separation. Residence proof of last 5-year residence in Delhi. Bank
account number (Single –operated) in Delhi only. Which is linked with Aadhar. Bank passbook/statement for the last year. In the case
of an SC/ST applicant, a certificate in the name of the applicant issued by a competent authority has to be submitted. In the case of
minority applicants self-declaration of the religion of the applicant duly verified by the religious institution has to be submitted. One
passport-size photo of the applicant. Income self-declaration (Format given on the portal can be used)
