## Details

The "Scheme of Personal Assistance Allowance to Differently Abled Persons with High Support Need" was launched by
the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to provide a personal
assistance allowance of ₹1,000/- to differently abled persons who need assistance for their activities of daily living. The applications are
accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the District Differently Abled Welfare Office.

## Benefits

Financial Assistance of
₹1,000/- per month. NOTE: The amount sent through ECS directly to the beneficiary’s bank account.
