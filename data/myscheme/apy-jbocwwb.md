## Eligibility

The deceased should have been domiciled in the
state of Jharkhand.The deceased should have been a Registered Worker with the 'Jharkhand Building and Other Construction Worker
Welfare Board (JBOCWW Board)'.The deceased should have been receiving a pension from the board.The applicant should be a child of
the deceased.The applicant should have been orphaned after the death of the pensioner.The applicant should be less than 18 years of
age.
