## Benefits

In this scheme, the government provides financial assistance of ₹ 10,000/- per annum to Self-owned
Auto/Taxi Drivers for expenditure towards insurance, fitness certificate, repairs, and other requirements.The payment will be made to the
bank account of the beneficiary, through either Bank Transfer or Aadhar Enabled Payment System (AePS).
