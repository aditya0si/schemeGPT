## Eligibility

Workers should be working in an establishment registered under the
Factories Act, 1948.The monthly salary of the worker (basic salary + dearness allowance) should not exceed ₹15,000/-.It is mandatory
to apply within one year from the date of death of the worker.Financial assistance will be payable to the worker's spouse or dependent
(son/unmarried daughter), mother/father in case the worker is unmarried.
