## Eligibility

Applicant should be a Permanent Resident of Punjab.Applicant should be Disabled and the Disability Percentage should
be more than 50%.The Annual Income of Family should not be more than Rs. 60,000/- per Year.People with mental disabilities are
eligible regardless of their specific disability.
