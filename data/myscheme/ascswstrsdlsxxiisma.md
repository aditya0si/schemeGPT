## Eligibility

The students should belong to the Scheduled Castes Category of Gujarat.The students who have
Secured Top Ranks at the State and District Levels in Gujarat in Standard X & Standard XII across all streams are eligible for cash
prizes.There is no income limit.

## FAQs

Who initiated this scheme?The scheme is initiated by the Director Scheduled
Caste Welfare, Department of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of Gujarat.What are the benefits of the scheme?Cash prizes
are awarded to students who secure the highest ranks in both Standard X and Standard XII across all streams at both state and district
levels. What are the award details for state-level rankers?For Standard X, the first three students receive ₹41,000/-, ₹21,000/-, and
₹11,000/-. For Standard XII, the prizes are ₹31,000/-, ₹21,000/-, and ₹11,000/- respectively.What are the award details for district-
level rankers?For Standard X, the first three students receive ₹6,000/-, ₹5,000/-, and ₹4,000/-. For Standard XII, the prizes are
₹6,000/-, ₹5,000/-, and ₹4,000/- respectively.Who is eligible for these cash prizes?Students belonging to the Scheduled Castes
Category of Gujarat who secure top ranks at both state and district levels in Standard X & XII exams are eligible.Are there any income
limits for eligibility?No, there is no income limit for eligibility under this scheme.How can students avail the benefits of this scheme?The
list of eligible students is obtained from the Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Board, and cash awards are given to selected
students accordingly.How are the winners selected?Winners are selected based on their rankings provided by the Gujarat Secondary and
Higher Secondary Board.
