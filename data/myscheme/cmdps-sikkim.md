## Details

The scheme “Chief Minister's State Disability Pension Scheme
(CMDPS)” was started by the Women & Child Welfare Department, Govt. of Sikkim to provide financial assistance to the differently
abled person who has a disability of 40% and above. This scheme is 100% State Innovative Scheme and the beneficiary will receive a
monthly pension of ₹ 1500/-.

## Benefits

The beneficiary will receive a monthly pension of ₹ 1500/-.
