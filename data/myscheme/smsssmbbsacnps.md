## Eligibility

A student
should fulfill the following criteria to be eligible for this Scholarship:The applicant/student should be an inhabitant of Nagaland.Students
selected through NEET/NSEE/State Departmental Examination only and undergoing medical, nursing and paramedical courses shall be
eligible to apply.Students failing to complete their curriculum within the specified duration of the course (repeaters) shall not be entitled
to an additional stipend.
