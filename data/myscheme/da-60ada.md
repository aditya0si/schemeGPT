## Details

"60% and Disability Allowance" is a pension scheme by the Directorate of Social Welfare and Social Education,
Government of Tripura. The scheme aims to provide financial assistance in the form of a monthly pension of ₹2,000/- to differently-
abled persons with a disability of 60% and above. The applicant should be submitted in person to the Child Development Project
Officer.

## Benefits

Pension of ₹2,000/- per month.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be a resident of Tripura.The applicant should be a
Person with Disability.The percentage of disability should be 60% and above.The applicant's age should be 10 years and above.The
family income of the applicant should be ₹1,00,000/- per annum.None of the family members of the applicant should be a government
employee.
