## Details

The scheme “Financial assistance to Women and
Girls Acid Attack Victims” was introduced by the Social Justice & Empowerment Department of the Govt. of Haryana on 26th Feb
2019. Any woman or girl victim/s facing an acid attack residing in the State of Haryana is eligible for financial benefit in this scheme.
Financial Assistance will be given from the current date after she has applied for the financial assistance under the scheme. “Acid
Attack Victim” here means a woman or girl who has suffered disfigurement of any part of the body or has become disabled due to an
acid attack or similar corrosive substance.

## Eligibility

The applicant must be a woman or a girl.The applicant must have
suffered disfigurement of any part of the body OR has become disabled due to an acid attack or similar corrosive substance.The acid
attack must have been on or after 2nd May 2011.The applicant must be a resident of Haryana and shall have been residing in Haryana for
at least 3 years prior to the date on which the acid attack took place. ExclusionsA girl / Woman who has self-inflicted the acid injuries
made advertently or inadvertently is not eligible for financial assistance under this scheme.

## Application Process

OfflineAn application
may be filed either by the victim or by her heir claiming on her behalf in cases where the victim herself is unable to make an application.
The victim herself, her parents, or her legal heirs may apply to the District Social Welfare Officer for relief. In the case of a minor
victim, the application may be made on her behalf by a parent or guardian. In the case of the victim being a married woman, the
application may be made on her behalf by her husband or parent, or guardian. The duly filled application form along with the self-
attested copies of the required documents must be submitted to the District Social Welfare Officer (DSWO).Documents
RequiredMedical certificate issued by the Medical Board at the District level where the application is being made by or on behalf of the
victim.Copy of the FIR/Complaint.Copy of application made to or benefit received from Women & Child Development
Department.
