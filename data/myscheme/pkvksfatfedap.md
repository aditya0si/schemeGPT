## Details

The scheme “rar Kamarajar Scheme of Financial
Assistance Towards Funeral Expenses of the Differently Abled Persons" was launched by the Department of Social Welfare,
Government of Puducherry. In this scheme, financial assistance of ₹ 15,000/- is provided to meet the expenses in connection with
funeral, in the event of the death of the differently abled person concerned.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be a Native/Resident of the Union Territory of
Puducherry for at least 5 years.The applicant should have a disability of 40% or above.The claimant should be son/daughter/spouse or
any other close relative under whose care and maintenance the differently abled person was living.The application shall be made within
30 days from the date of death of the differently abled person.
