## Eligibility

The applicant should be the Women and Children who are victims of
Commercial Sexual Exploitation.The applicant should be either residing in Goa or be a native of Goa.NOTE 1: The place of commercial
sexual exploitation is not a criterion for eligibility (women and girls from Goa being commercially sexually exploited outside Goa also
qualify).
