## Eligibility

The beneficiary must be a resident of Karnataka. They must have a valid resident certificate.All the weavers and
financially distressed workers engaged in the handloom/ power loom industry and ancillary activities such as cotton, wool, and silk in the
state are eligible. 
Prospective beneficiaries must have an active bank account.
