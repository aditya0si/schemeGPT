## Benefits

Under the scheme, the registered worker who has been working as a building
worker for not less than one year shall receive an amount of ₹1,000/- per mensem as pension.

## Eligibility

1. The beneficiary should be a
resident of Meghalaya.2. The beneficiary should be engaged in a building or other construction work.3. The beneficiary should be
registered under the Meghalaya Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.4. The registered workers who have been
working as building workers for not less than one year shall be eligible to receive pension benefits under the scheme.5. The registered
worker shall become eligible to avail pension benefits upon attaining the age of 60 years.
