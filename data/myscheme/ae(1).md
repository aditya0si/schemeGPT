## Benefits

Of The SchemeTo supplement efforts of public extension by necessarily providing extension and other
services to the farmers on a payment basis or free of cost as per the business model of agri-preneur, local needs and affordability of the
target group of farmers. To support agricultural developmentTo create gainful self-employment opportunities for unemployed
agricultural graduates, agricultural diploma holders, intermediate in agriculture and biological science graduates, with PG in agri-related
courses
