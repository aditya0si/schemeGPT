## Details

The
Self Employment Scheme for Rehabilitation of Manual Scavengers (SRMS) was introduced in January 2017 by the Ministry of Social
Justice and Empowerment, to assist the identified manual scavengers and their dependants in rehabilitation in alternative occupations.
SRMS was revised in November 2013 in consonance with the provision of the “Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers
and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013 (MS Act, 2013).
