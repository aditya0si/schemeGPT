## Details

The 'Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme' is a state scheme launched by
the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme aims to provide a pension of
₹1,000/- to the destitute widows who are 40 years and above in age and belong to households below the poverty line. The widow is
also provided with one free saree during Pongal and Deepavali, and free rice in the Anganwadi Centres.
