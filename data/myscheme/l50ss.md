## Eligibility

The applicant should be a citizen of India.The applicant should be a permanent resident of Maharashtra State.The applicant
should be in the 18 to 50 years age group.The applicant should be from Charmakar Community only (Dhor, Chambhar, Holar, Mochi,
etc.).The annual income of the family of the applicant should be less than or equal to ₹1,00,000/-.The applicant should have knowledge
of the business for which he has applied for the loan.
