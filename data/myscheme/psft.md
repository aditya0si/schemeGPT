## Details

Launched on 1st August 2015, the scheme "Pension Scheme for Transgender" is a Pension Scheme by the
Directorate of Social Welfare and Social Education, Government of Tripura. The scheme provides financial assistance of ₹2,000/- to
the transgender residents of Tripura who have no source of income.

## Benefits

₹2,000/- per month.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be a
resident of Tripura.The applicant should be Transgender.
