## Benefits

The fellowship amount under this programme is ₹37,000/- per month along with House Rent Allowances (HRA) as per
norms.This Programme also provides a Research Grant of ₹1.00 lakh per year and Institutional overhead charges @₹ 0.50 lakh per
year (as per DST norms).

## Eligibility

Unemployed women who want to pursue a Ph.D. can apply for a fellowship under WISE-PhD. The
programme is open for both candidates; those who have not yet registered as well as those who have already registered for a Ph.D., but
not availing of a fellowship from any sources. However, candidates who have completed two (2) years duration in Ph.D. as of the date of
application are NOT eligible. The following are the primary eligibility criteria:Qualification:The applicant should have an M. Sc./ M.
Tech. or equivalent degree like B. Tech., M.Phil., M. Pharm. etc. in basic and applied sciences from a recognized
University/Institute.Age:The minimum age to apply for the programme is 27 years and the maximum is 45 years (A relaxation of 3 years
in the upper age limit is allowed for SC/ ST/ PH candidates). The cut-off date for age calculation would be the date of submission of the
WISE-PhD application.Employment: Women in regular employment are not eligible for WISE-PhD.
