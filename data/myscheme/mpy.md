## Eligibility

The applicant should be a domicile in the state of Jharkhand.The applicant
should be a female.The applicant should be pregnant.The applicant should be a Worker registered with the 'Jharkhand Building and
Other Construction Worker Welfare Board (JBOCWW Board)'.The applicant should be between 18-60 years of age.The applicant should
be engaged in construction work such as masons, carpenters, electricians, porters, painters, etc.The scheme is applicable only for the first
