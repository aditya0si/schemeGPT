## Details

The
"Reactivation Of Fisheries Cooperative Societies" Component of the "Mukhyamantri Maschyajibi Kalyan Yojana" Scheme was
launched by the Fisheries & Animal Resources Development Department, Government of Odisha. The objective of this scheme is to
provide a one-time revolving fund of ₹2,00,000/- to each eligible Primary Fishermen Cooperative Society for working capital of the
society and improvement of the socio-economic status of the members in the long run. The scheme shall be implemented by the
Directorate of Fisheries through the respective District Fisheries Officer.
