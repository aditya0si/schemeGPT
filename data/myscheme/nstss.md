## Details

The National Sports Talent Search Scheme (NSTSS).Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports
Department of Sports. Provided e, Identification of sporting talent among students all over the country in the age group of 8 – 12 years
(for admission in Class IV to Class VI) who possess inborn qualities such as anthropometric, physical and physiological capabilities
without any anatomical infirmities.In the second phase, Nurturing of the Sporting Potential talent identified in District level Sports
schools / Central Sports Schools / Junior Sports Academies etc., to make them excel at the National and international sports
competitions. ObjectiveIdentify budding athletes with exceptional potential. Provide them with quality training and development
opportunities. Prepare them for future success in national and international competitions

## Benefits

The program will help in finding
athletes with natural abilities like anthropometric, physical, and physiological capacities without any anatomical defects among children
for admission in Class IV to Class VI.The program will support the growth of Indian sports, especially rural sports.Sports schools at the
district, regional, and national levels as well as national and international sports academies will foster the development of athletes’
sporting potential and talent. As a result of this, the number of athletes in the nation will increase.
