## Eligibility

The girl student should belong to the Saharia tribe of the Baran district of Rajasthan.The girl child should
be studying regularly in a government school.The mother/father/guardian/guardian of the student should not be an income taxpayer.The
girl students, who are studying in residential schools/ashram hostels run by the government, they will not be entitled.Vaidya Bhamashah
card and Aadhaar should be there.Students should be a native of Rajasthan.
