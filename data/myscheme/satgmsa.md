## Eligibility

A student should fulfill the following criteria to be eligible for this
Scholarship:The State Agriculture Thesis Grant will be applicable for all the inhabitants of Nagaland who passed the M.Sc.
Agriculture.Applicants must be from an Institution which has a valid AISHE/UDISE code.Applicants must not be a beneficiary of any
other scholarship scheme.
