## Details

The
"Poompuhar State Award" scheme was launched by the Department of Handlooms, Handicrafts, Textiles & Khadi, Government of Tamil
Nadu. The best artisans who have contributed to the development of the crafts of Tamil Nadu are selected and given a cash prize of
₹50,000/, a 4-gram gold medal, tamirapathiram and a certificate. The economically backward awardees are also eligible for a monthly
pension of ₹3,500/- after completion of 60 years of age.
