## Eligibility

The applicant should be from a Scheduled Caste.The applicant should be a student
studying at the Post-Matric level or Post Secondary Stage.The applicant should be a Native/Resident of the Union Territory of
Puducherry for not less than 5 years.The annual income should not exceed ₹ 2,00,000/-.
