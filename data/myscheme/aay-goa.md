## Details

"Atal Asra Yojana" by the Department of Tribal Welfare, Government of Goa provides financial assistance of up to ₹
3,00,000 to individuals from Scheduled Tribes communities for construction/ re-construction and repairs of the house.

## Benefits

Financial
Assistance of ₹ 3,00,000 for the construction of a new house/re-construction of the existing house.Financial Assistance of ₹ 1,50,000
for the repair of the existing house.
