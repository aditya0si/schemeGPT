## Details

The scheme “Assistance under SC/ST Prevention of Atrocities Act”
was introduced by the Adi Dravidar Welfare Department, Government of Puducherry. The objective of the scheme is to provide
immediate relief to the victims of atrocities who are from Scheduled Castes/ Scheduled Tribes.
