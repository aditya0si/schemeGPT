## Details

The
scheme “Allowance for 100% Blind" is a Pension Scheme launched by the Directorate of Social Welfare and Social Education,
Government of Tripura. The scheme aims to provide a monthly pension of ₹2,000/- to the blind persons of Tripura aged 10 years and
above having 100% blindness. The application should be submitted to the nearest Child Development Project Officer
(CDPO).

## Benefits

Pension of ₹2,000/- per month.*The existing beneficiaries who are on receipt of benefits under the scheme can
continue to do so.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be a resident of Tripura.The applicant should be 100% visually handicapped.The
applicant's age should be 10 years and above. The annual income of the applicant's family should be up to ₹ 1,00,000/-.ExclusionsNo
member of the applicant's family should be a government employee.
