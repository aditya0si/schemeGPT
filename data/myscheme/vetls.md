## Benefits

Quantum of loan Need-based finance to meet up to 90% of
expenses shall be considered subject to the following Ceilings:For courses of duration 
up to 2 years: up to Rs. 4,00,000/-Note: In case of
cost over-run, it shall be borne by the beneficiary.Interest National Safai Karamcharis Finance & Development Corporation (NSKFDC)
shall charge interest @ 1% per annum from the Channelizing Agency which, in turn, shall charge 4% per annum from the beneficiaries.
In the case of women, an interest rebate of 0.5% shall be provided. 
Repayment: Up to 7 years.

## Eligibility

The candidate should attain a minimum age of 18 years on completion of the
course. The upper age limit is up to 50 years.
