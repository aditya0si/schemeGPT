## Eligibility

The parents of the girl should be permanent
residents of Punjab.Girls should be born after 1.1.2011.Abundant Girls were found after 1.1.2011 and are residing in orphanages and
Children's Homes in the Punjab State.This benefit will be given to those families whose annual income is less than Rs.30,000/- and the
proof of income will be the blue card issued by the Food and Supply Department, Punjab.The number of girls born before will not matter
to get benefits under the scheme. If the girl child drops from school due to any reason then no benefit will be given to the beneficiaries or
the families after that date.ExclusionsExclusionsThis benefit will not be given to newly born boys. This benefit will not be given to newly
