## Eligibility

Applicant
must be resident of West Bengal State.Applicant must be 60 years old or above, but in the case of physically and mentally handicapped
individuals, the age limit for eligibility is 55 years.The person should not have any relation capable of providing financial support. Here
“relation” means and includes-Son, Grand Son.Husband, Wife.Unmarried daughter.Parents.The income of the person should not
exceed ₹ 1,000/- per month.The applicant should not be a recipient of any other Government pension.Residency DurationThe disabled
person should have been a resident of the state of West Bengal for at least 10 years at the time of applying for the pension.
(OR)Exception: The State Government may relax the residency condition in cases where undue hardship is
caused.ExclusionsProfessional Beggars and Mendicants:Individuals engaged in begging as a profession are not eligible for pension under
this scheme.Individuals who do not beg professionally but receive occasional assistance are eligible if they meet other
criteria. Recipients of Other Pensions:Disabled persons already receiving "Widow Pension," "Old Age Pension," "Farmers’
Pension," or pension under the "Family Pension Scheme" from the State Government, Central Government, or other concerns are not
entitled to the pension under this scheme.
