## Benefits

Under the scheme, a one-time financial assistance of ₹35,000/- will be
provided to women of BPL families to find self-employment, whose husbands have died/abandoned, and who have to take care of their
sons/children with severe physical and mental challenges.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be a native and resident of Kerala State.The
applicant should be from Below Poverty Line (BPL) family.The parents (mothers) with children having 70% or more disability / mental
retardation are eligible to apply under the scheme.Single/Unwed mothers/Wives abandoned by husbands/Legally divorced
women/Separated women are eligible to apply under this scheme.Close relatives (women- as mentioned in the above category) who act
as caretakers of severely disabled/mentally challenged patients can also apply.Note 01: Mothers of severely disabled/mentally challenged
bedridden patients will be given priority.Note 02: Beneficiaries of the “Aswasakiranam Scheme” can also apply under the
scheme.
