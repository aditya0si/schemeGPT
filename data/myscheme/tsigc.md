## Details

An Incentive
Scheme by the Directorate Of Social Welfare & Social Education, Govt. of Tripura, for the girl child born on or after 01/06/2009 to the
parents who are cardholders of listed BPL households. The incentive will be provided at the rate of ₹300 per head per month up to the
month the girl child attains the age of 15 years. The incentive amount will be paid to the mother of the girl child, preferably to the
bank/post office account. Up to two girl children in a family will be covered by the scheme. The CDPO will sanction the incentive with
prior approval of the Director of Social Welfare and Social Education.
