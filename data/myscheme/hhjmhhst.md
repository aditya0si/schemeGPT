## Details

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme
"Hamaal Hetu Joota, Huk Evan Mahila Hamaal Hetu Soopa Aur Tokari Sahaayata Yojana" for unorganized worker on 
11 March 2015.
The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing financial assistance

## Benefits

For shoe, hook for lifting
sack for Hamal and soup and basket for female Hamal : 
₹ 500/- per beneficiary.

## Eligibility

Applicant should be registered in any district
of the state.If applicant received from any other parallel scheme of the State Government, then applicant is not eligible for this
scheme.Beneficiaries aged between 18 years to 60 years will be eligible under the scheme.
