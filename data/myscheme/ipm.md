## Details

The “Plant Protection including
Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Scheme” is a State Plan Scheme, which is implemented by the Department of Agriculture &
Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya. The objective of the scheme is to mobilize and support farmers to use bio-pesticides
and bio- agents for plant health management through the distribution of bio-pesticides and other IPM tools and through the sale of
Sprayers and Plant Protection Equipment to encourage organic crop production.
