## Details

The "Scheme of Giving Awards to Eminent Artists - Kalai Ilamani Award" is a
state scheme launched by the Art and Culture Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme's objective is to promote and
preserve the culture of Tamil Nadu. The selected artists are awarded financial assistance ₹4,000/- and certificates of appreciation. The
scheme is being implemented in all Districts of Tamil Nadu.

## Benefits

Financial Assistance of ₹4,000/-.Certificate of Appreciation.A
shawl worth ₹500/-.5 eminent artists are awarded every year.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be an artist.The applicant's age should be
18 years and below.
