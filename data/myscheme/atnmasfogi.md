## Details

The 'Annai Teresa Ninaivu Marriage Assistance Scheme for Orphan Girls-I' was launched by the
Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Government of Tamil Nadu. The scheme provides financial assistance to the
orphan girls for their marriage. The bride should have completed 18 years of age at the time of marriage.

## Benefits

Financial assistance of
₹25,000/-.One sovereign (8gm) 22-carat gold coin for making Thirumangalyam. Mode of Disbursal: Through Electronic Clearing
Service.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be a girl.The applicant should be an orphan.The bride should be 18 years or above at the time of
marriage.
