## Benefits

Financial incentive of
Rs.500/- per month for each notified TB patient for duration for which the patient is on anti-TB treatment

## Eligibility

All patients with
tuberculosis (TB) who are notified to the government on or after April 1, 2018, including those who are already being treated for TB, are
eligible for financial incentives.To be eligible for the incentives, patients must be registered or notified on the NIKSHAY
portal.

## Application Process

OnlineTo register on the NIKSHAY portal, please follow these steps:Visit the NIKSHAY portal website.Click
on the "Register" button.Enter your personal information, such as your name, email address, and phone number.Enter your healthcare
facility information, such as the name and address of your facility.Create a password and confirm it.Click on the "Register" button.Once
you have registered, you will receive an email with a verification link. Click on the link to verify your account.Once your account has
been verified, you can log in to the portal and start notifying TB cases to the government.To notify a TB case, follow these steps:Click on
the "Notify TB Case" button.Enter the patient's personal information, such as their name, date of birth, and address.Enter the patient's TB
diagnosis information, such as the type of TB and the date of diagnosis.Enter the patient's treatment information, such as the start date of
treatment and the type of treatment.Click on the "Submit" button.The government will then verify the information and process the
incentive payment.Please note that all TB cases must be notified to the government through the NIKSHAY portal in order to be eligible
for the incentive payment.
