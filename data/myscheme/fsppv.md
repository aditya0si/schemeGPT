## Details

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this
scheme "Futkar Sabji, Phal-Phool Vikreta Hetu Taraju, Baat Evan Tokari Sahaayata Yojana" for unorganized worker on 15 June 2016..
The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing financial assistance of Cycle or Torch .

## Benefits

An
amount of Rs 700/- (Rs seven hundred only) per beneficiary will be provided for scales, weights and 02 baskets.

## Eligibility

Applicant
must be registered as an unorganized worker in any district of the state.Applicant must have minimum age of 18 years. Applicant should
not have received the similar benefits from any other schemes. ExclusionsApplicant should not have received the similar benefits from
any other schemes.
