## Details

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this scheme "Asangathit Karmakar
Samachar-Patr Hawker Cycle Sahayata Yojana" for unorganized worker on 10 September 2010. Under this scheme, One cycle will be
provided free of cost to each unorganized 
beneficiary. The main objective of the scheme to empower the unorganized Samaachaar-Patr
Hokar worker through provide them free cycle.

## Benefits

A free of cost cycle will be provided.

## Eligibility

This scheme is for unorganized
newspaper delivery workers (hawker) registered in any district of the state.Newspaper delivery workers (hawker) are not eligible for this
scheme if they receive benefits from a parallel scheme.
