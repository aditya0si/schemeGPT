## Details

The "Assistance to Law Graduates" scheme was launched by the Department for the Welfare of
Differently Abled Persons, Government of Tamil Nadu. Under this scheme, financial assistance of ₹50,000/- will be given to the
differently abled Law graduates for enrolment in the Bar Council as lawyer and for the purchase of law books.

## Benefits

Financial
Assistance of ₹50,000/-.
