## Details

The Mukhya Mantri Kanyadan Yojana in Himachal Pradesh is a welfare scheme aimed at
providing financial assistance for the marriage of daughters from economically weaker sections of society. Under this scheme, daughters
of families below the poverty line, widows, destitute women, and families with an annual income of less than Rs. 35,000 are eligible for
financial assistance from the government for their marriage. The scheme aims to reduce the financial burden on families during the
marriage of their daughters and promote gender equality. The scheme has been successful in providing financial support to many
families, particularly those from underprivileged backgrounds, and helping them to celebrate the marriage of their daughters with dignity
and joy.
