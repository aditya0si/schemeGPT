## Details

Widow/ Deserted/ Ekal Nari Pension Rs. 700 per month is being
provided to those Widow/ Deserted/ Ekal Nari ladies above 45 years of age whose annual income does not exceed Rs. 35,000 per
year. The scheme aims to improve the quality of life of these women who are unable to support themselves financially. Under the
scheme, eligible women receive a monthly pension of Rs. 700, subject to the fulfillment of certain criteria.
