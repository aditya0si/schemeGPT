## Details

"Chief Ministergi Shotharabasingi Tengbang (CMST): Scholarship of Students with Disabilities" is a Scholarship
Scheme by the Social Welfare Department, Govt. of Manipur. In this scheme, the Students with Disability (SwD) who are a domicile of
the State of Manipur, and are studying in Class 1st and above are awarded with scholarship of upto ₹ 8,000/-. The student must be
enrolled in a school/college/university/institution recognized by the Govt. of Manipur. The amount of the scholarhip shall be disbursed
directly into the bank account of the beneficiary.

## Benefits

Class: 1st to 5th; Rate of Scholarship (per annum): ₹ 3,000/-.Class: 6th to 8th;
Rate of Scholarship (per annum): ₹ 4,000/-.Class: 9th to 12th; Rate of Scholarship (per annum): ₹ 6,000/-.Graduate & Above
(including all Technical Courses): ₹ 8,000/-.
