## Details

The scheme "Indira Gandhi Utkrisht Chattervriti Yojana for Post
Plus Two Students" is a Scholarship Scheme by the Department of Education, Government of Himachal Pradesh. Through this scheme, a
scholarship of ₹ 18,000/- per annum will be awarded to 10 meritorious students each from Class 12 (Arts, Science, and Commerce) and
from the merit list of B.A., B.Sc. and B.Com. degree. The scholarship will be renewed yearly until they complete the
degree/course.
