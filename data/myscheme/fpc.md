## Details

The scheme “Fruit Processing Centre” was launched
by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Government of Meghalaya to disseminate technical knowhow to youth
entrepreneurs, farmers, and housewives through the practical demonstration on the importance of fruit preservation and to manufacture
quality processed fruit products such as Squashes, Jams, Jelly, canned fruits and Juices under the trade name ‘Meg Fruits
Products’.

## Benefits

Pattern of Assistance:Training and DemonstrationType of Assistance:Awareness, Capacity building on fruit and
vegetable processing

## Eligibility

The applicant should be a permanent resident of Meghalaya.The applicant should be a bonafide farmer
engaged in agriculture.
