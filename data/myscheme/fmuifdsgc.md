## Benefits

Under this scheme, the fodder seed minikit of 3 Kg, 7 Kg, or 10 Kg is given to the eligible
beneficiary.

## Eligibility

The beneficiary should be a permanent resident of Gujarat.Any beneficiary of the General category can avail the
benefits under the scheme.The beneficiary should possess 10 Guntha of land to his or her name.
