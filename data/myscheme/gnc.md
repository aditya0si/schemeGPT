## Details

The "Gen
Next Competition" scheme was launched by the Handlooms, Handicrafts, Textiles, and Khadi Dept, Government of Tamil Nadu. The
scheme aims to ensure that the traditional handicrafts of Tamil Nadu survive and are passed on to the next generation, it is necessary to
enthuse the youngsters in the field. To achieve this, a craft competition was introduced from the year 2013-2014 onwards for artisans
below 30 years of age.

## Benefits

Cash Prize of ₹2,000/-.Silver Medal. Certificate.

The applicant should be an Artisan.The
