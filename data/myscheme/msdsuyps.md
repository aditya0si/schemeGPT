## Details

Launched on 9th
February 2024, the scheme "Mukhyamantri Sports Development Scheme - Unemployed Young Professional Sportsperson" was
launched by the Directorate of Youth Affairs & Sports, Government of Tripura. The scheme aims to generate employment opportunities
for young experienced professional sportspersons (Junior Coach) to give a monthly fixed remuneration of ₹20,000/-. Duration: The
contract is tenable for a maximum period of 12 months, it may be extended or curtailed

## Benefits

Fixed Remuneration of ₹20,000/- per
month.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be a resident of Tripura.The applicant should be a young sportsperson.The applicant should be an
experienced professional.The age of the applicant should be between 18 to 40 years (a relaxation of 5 years in the upper age limit for
ST/SC candidates).
