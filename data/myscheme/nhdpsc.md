## Benefits

Financial
Coverage: The scholarship covers tuition fees, admission fees, examination fees, and other annual fees/charges as required by the
academic institution.Stipend: In addition to the academic fees, eligible candidates will receive a stipend of ₹5,000 per month, subject to
a maximum of ₹2,00,000 per child per annum or actuals, whichever is less.Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT): Funds will be directly
transferred to the bank account of the concerned handloom weaver/worker or their children through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
system. Payment ScheduleThe scholarship for the first year will be disbursed after the admission of the ward of handloom
weavers/workers.Subsequent years' scholarships will be paid upon submission of the proposal, along with a copy of the mark sheet for
the annual exam, and admission proof for the next academic session from the relevant institution.

## Eligibility

The scholarship is for the
children of handloom weavers/workers (up to 2 children).This support is available for pursuing 3/4 years Diploma/Graduate/Post
Graduate courses at Central/State Government recognized and funded Textiles Institutions.
