## Benefits

Financial Assistance: Limit of funding ₹ 4,00,000/- to the institution.Disbursement of the Fund: The full
amount of the grant sanctioned shall be released after successful conduct of the program to Indian Micro Enterprises Development
Foundation (IMEDF).The amount spent by the institute on the conduct of STTP-SFURTI shall be adjusted on the basis of utilization
certificate and detailed expenditure statement submitted by the IMEDF on the prescribed format along with other mandatory documents
viz feedback form, copy of proceedings and completion report etc
