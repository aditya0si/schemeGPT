## Details

Launched on 1st August 2015, the scheme "Pension Scheme for Grade II Deformed Leprosy
Patients" is a Pension Scheme by the Directorate of Social Welfare and Social Education, Government of Tripura. The scheme provides
financial assistance of ₹2,000/- per month to Leprosy Patients who are residents of Tripura. The applications are accepted
offline.

## Benefits

Pension of ₹2,000/- per month.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be a resident of Tripura. The applicant should
be affected with Leprosy Grade II.
