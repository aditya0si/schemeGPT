## Eligibility

Applicant
must be a widow women from Rajasthan State.Applicant must be resident of Rajasthan or living in Rajasthan for 3 years before re-
marriage.Applicant should be entitled to pension as per widow pension rules.Applicant age should be between 18-50 years.If the
applicant has remarried before this scheme then he/she will not be eligible.
