## Benefits

1. The registered worker will be
provided ₹3000/- for the purchase of the bicycle.

## Eligibility

1. The worker should be registered under Haryana Building and Other
Construction Workers Welfare Board (HBOCWWB).2. The registered worker should have a minimum of one-year regular
membership.3. The worker may avail of the benefits only once in 3 years. 
Note: The beneficiary needs to submit an undertaking for the
purchase of the bicycle giving the price, trademark, source, and date.
