## Details

The scheme ial Association, CII, Industrial NGOs for
Promotion of SME Clusters' under 'Marketing And Publicity' 
was launched by the Department of Industries and Commerce, Union
Territory of Puducherry. Through this scheme, financial assistance is provided to the Industrial Associations, CII, and Industrial NGOs
for the promotion of the SME Cluster. The financial assistance is 75% of the actual cost, subject to a maximum of ₹3,00,000/- per unit
in cluster.
