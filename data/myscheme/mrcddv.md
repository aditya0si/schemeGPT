## Details

Labor Department of Chhattisgarh State started this
scheme "Mukhyamantri Raut, Charavaaha Evan Doodh Duhane Vaale Bicycle Sahaayata Yojana" for unorganized worker on 10
September 2013. The main objective of the scheme to empower unorganized worker by providing financial assistance.

## Benefits

Financial
Assistance of ₹ 3,000/- per person for bicycle or the rate fixed by CSIDC for bicycle which will be lower
