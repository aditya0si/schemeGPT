## Details

.CM Maha Lakshmi Scheme has seen quite a few twists and turns since it was announced by former CM Trivendra
Rawat a couple of months ago. He had named the scheme “Soubhagyawati Yojana” and was all set to inaugurate it when he had to
step down. His successor, Tirath Singh Rawat changed the name of the scheme to Mahalakshmi Yojana and was gearing up to launch it.
But on the very day, he was to inaugurate Maha Laxmi Yojana, he was summoned to Delhi and subsequently had to let go of the
CM’s chair. Mukhyamantri Mahalaxmi Yojana has been launched in Uttarakhand. CM Pushkar Singh Dhami launched this
scheme at CM Camp office. CM Dhami and Women Empowerment and Child Development Minister Rekha Arya distributed
Mahalaxmi kits to the beneficiary women. At the same time, the beneficiary women from different districts also joined the virtual
program where Mahalaxmi kits were distributed through the district magistrates. Under the Mukhyamantri Mahalaxmi Yojana, a total of
16929 beneficiaries have been benefited in the first phase in all the districts of the entire state. More than 50 thousand people are to be
benefited in a year. CM Pushkar Singh Dhami said that the benefit of this scheme will also be seen in the improvement of gender
ratio
