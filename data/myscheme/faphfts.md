## Benefits

Under the scheme, financial assistance of ₹4,000/- per month
will be provided to Transgender students as rent for finding hostel facilities/accommodation during the course.

## Eligibility

1. The applicant
should be Transgender.2. The applicant should be a resident of Kerala.3. Transgender students pursuing courses in Government / Aided
/Un-aided / Self-financing institutions can apply under this scheme.Note: During the commencement of each academic year the students
have to submit a new application for getting the benefit of this scheme.
