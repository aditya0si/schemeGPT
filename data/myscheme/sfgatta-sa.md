## Details

"Scheme for Giving Awards to Traditional Artists" was launched by the Art and Culture Department,
Government of Tamil Nadu. Through this scheme, awards are conferred to the participant artists whose works are adjudged outstanding
among the entries at the State-level Traditional Art Exhibition conducted by the Kuzhu. The awarded works are put on display for public
view in the exhibition. The applications are accepted offline in the prescribed form available at the Oviya Nunkalai Kuzhu.

## Benefits

Prize
Money of ₹5,000/- per artist is given to 10 artists.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be an artist.The artist should be a native of Tamil
Nadu by continuous residence for not less than 5 years.The artist should be in the field of creating artworks like tanjore paintings, glass
paintings, traditional paintings, wood carvings, metal sculptures, etc.
