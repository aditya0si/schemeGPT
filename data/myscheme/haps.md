## Details

The Government of Puducherry introduced the HIV / AIDS Pension Scheme to extend a
helping hand through monthly financial assistance to HIV/AIDS Affected People so that they could defray some petty personal/ medical
expenses without depending on others.

## Benefits

₹ 1500/- per month with age less than 60 years.₹ 2000/- per month with age between
60 years and 79 years.₹ 3000/- per month with age 80 years and above.
