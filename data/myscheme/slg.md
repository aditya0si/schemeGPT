Law Graduates belonging to the SC/ST Community residing in the state of

Advocates.Monthly stipend of Rs 5000 is given for a period of 4 years.The stipend is given to an SC/ST resident of Karnataka, who is a
law degree holder from any State or Central government recognized University / Institution. The total income of the Applicant should be
less than Rs. 2.0 lakhs. The following are the eligibility criteria for this scheme Applicant should be a resident of Karnataka
state.Applicant should belong to the
