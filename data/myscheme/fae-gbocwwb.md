## Details

"Financial Assistance for Education (GBOCWWB)" is a Welfare Scheme by the Goa Building
and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board of the Department of Labour and Employment, Government of Goa. Through this
scheme, the worker registered with the Board is provided with financial assistance, as determined by the Board, for the education of their
children. The applications are accepted offline.

## Benefits

Financial Assistance, as determined by the Board, for the education of the
children of the Worker.

## Eligibility

The applicant should be a Worker registered with the Goa Building and Other Construction Workers
Welfare Board.The worker should be between 18 to 60 years of age.The worker should have completed ninety days of service as a
registered worker in the year.The ward of the worker should be pursuing education.
