## Benefits

The scheme aims to provide
affordable and nutritious meals to farmers and their helpers at a subsidized rate of ₹5/- per plate.The meals will be provided in the
mandi premises itself.The menu for the meal includes:8 chapatis (made from 250 grams of flour).1 bowl of dal (125 grams).1 bowl of
vegetables (125 grams).50 grams of jaggery (in winter, from October to March).200 ml of buttermilk (in summer, from April to
September).
