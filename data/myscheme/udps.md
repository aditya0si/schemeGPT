## Details

Dwarf Pension Scheme is started by the state Government of Uttarakhand for Dwarf people. The
dwarf person has to face difficulty in living his life in a social environment. Sometimes, it’s not easy for getting employment for
Dwarf people. 
The main purpose of the scheme is to provide pensions and connect the dwarf people of the Uttarakhand state to the
mainstream of society. The main aim of this scheme is to provide social security to the Dwarf people of the state who are unable to
sustain themselves from their own resources. So, this scheme will provide a better livelihood and make their life easy. In this scheme,
dwarf below 4 feet can eligible for getting benefits of the pension. The Uttarakhand government took a very good initiative for the Dwarf
people.

## Benefits

The government provides financial assistance of Rs. 1200/- per month.
