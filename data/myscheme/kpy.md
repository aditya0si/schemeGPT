## Details

The main
objective of the Uttarakhand Pension Yojana is to provide financial assistance to all the needy citizens of the state. Through this scheme,
all the eligible citizens of Uttarakhand will be able to maintain themselves properly and their life will also improve. Citizens of
Uttarakhand will become self-reliant through Uttarakhand Pension Yojana. All the citizens coming below the poverty line can take
advantage of the Uttarakhand Pension Yojana. Now through this scheme, the beneficiaries will not have to face problems in living.
Money is transferred to the beneficiary's account through direct bank transfer.Under the Uttarakhand Kisan Pension Yojana, financial
assistance is provided to the old farmers of the state. To take advantage of this scheme, the age of the farmer should be more than 60
years. This financial assistance is Rs 14,400 per year. Which is provided by the government in two instalments at an interval of 6-6
months. So far Rs 1539 crore has been spent under this scheme.

## Benefits

This financial assistance is Rs 14,400 per
year.

## Eligibility

The applicant must be a permanent resident of Uttarakhand.The applicant's age should be more than 60
years.The applicant should not get pension benefits from any other sources.The Applicant should have a maximum of 2 hectares of land
and do farming on the same.
