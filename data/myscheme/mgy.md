## Eligibility

The applicant should belong to the Scheduled Caste category.The applicant should be a native of Gujarat
state.The age limit of the applicant should be 18 to 60 years.The applicant from the Scheduled Caste Category should have an annual
family income of up to ₹6,00,000/-.There is no income limit for applicants belonging to the Most Backward Caste among the
Scheduled Castes category.If the beneficiary or any family members of the beneficiary have already benefited from this scheme, they are
not eligible to avail of its benefits again.

## FAQs

What does the scheme aim to provide?The scheme offers financial
assistance and employment/tool kits to Scheduled Caste individuals who aspire to establish cottage industries for self-employment,
without relying on bank loans.What are the benefits offered under the scheme?Tool kits are provided for 28 types of trades/businesses,
including handicraft work, tailoring, vehicle servicing, and carpentry, among others.Who is eligible to apply for the scheme?Applicants
must belong to the Scheduled Caste category, be natives of Gujarat, and aged between 18 to 60 years.Is there an income limit for
applicants?Yes, applicants from the Scheduled Caste category must have an annual family income of up to ₹6,00,000/-. However, there
is no income limit for applicants belonging to the Most Backward Caste among the Scheduled Castes.Can previous beneficiaries or their
family members reapply for the scheme?No, if the beneficiary or any family members have already availed benefits under the scheme,
they are not eligible for reapplication.Where can eligible applicants apply for the scheme?Eligible applicants can apply through the e-
Samaj Kalyan Portal: https://esamajkalyan.gujarat.gov.in/How many types of trades/businesses are covered under the scheme?A total of
28 types of trades/businesses are covered under the scheme, ranging from handicraft work to mobile repairing.Can applicants from
outside Gujarat apply for the scheme?No, only native residents of Gujarat are eligible to apply for the "Manav Garima Yojana"
scheme.What is the significance of providing employment/tool kits?Providing employment/tool kits ensures that individuals have the
necessary resources to start their businesses, reducing dependency on external sources like bank loans.
