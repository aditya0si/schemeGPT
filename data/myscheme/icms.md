## Details

The Inter-caste Marriage Scheme is an initiative by the Government of Himachal Pradesh to
promote inter-caste marriages and encourage social integration. Under this scheme, financial assistance of up to Rs. 75,000 is provided to
couples belonging to different castes who get married. The scheme aims to break down caste barriers and promote social
harmony.
