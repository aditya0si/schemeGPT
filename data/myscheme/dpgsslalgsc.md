## Benefits

Under the scheme, A Law graduates belonging to Scheduled Caste intending to start legal practice will
be given ₹12,000/-; out of which ₹7,000/- will be @4% interest loan, and ₹5,000/- will be a subsidy.The loan is provided to purchase
business premises/shops.
