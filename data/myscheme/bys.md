## Details

DetailThe Karnataka state government will give female children financial aid under the Karnataka Bhagya Lakshmi Scheme.
The payment of this support is to be made through the mother, father, or legal guardian provided that all requirements are met. The
objective of this schemeTo promote the birth of girl children among families identified as below the poverty line.To increase the status of
girl child thereby raising the status of the society.To Promote the birth of girl child in economically weaker families and to raise their
status within the family and society.To provide Financial assistance to the girl child through her mother/father/natural guardian subject to
the fulfilment of certain conditions
