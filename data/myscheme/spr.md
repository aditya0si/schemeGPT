## Details

The Super 5000
scheme is a part of the ongoing Jan Kalyan Sambal Yojana in Madhya Pradesh. Under this, an education incentive will be given to the
first 5000 students of unorganized sector workers who get the maximum marks in the 10th and 12th board examinations. Meritorious
students will be able to use this amount to continue their further studies.
