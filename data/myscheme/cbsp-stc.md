## Eligibility

For the Trainee (before enrolment) - The applicant should have passed Class
8th.The applicant should be above the age of 18 years. For the Trainee (after enrolment) - To ensure that training are viewed seriously,
a trainee must attend 80% of the classes/periods to become eligible for a certificate/ for appearing in the end-of-the-course test. Note 1:
The batch size of all programs/courses requiring hands-on/practical training should ideally comprise 20 personsNote 2: The selection of
trainees is to be done in a transparent manner and the same trainee or person should not be selected again.Note 3: The students and
trainers to be enrolled must be linked to Aadhar in the Implementing Agency’s records as well as the individual’s Banks account.
 Reservation / Priority / PreferenceWhile selecting candidates, it should be ensured that the majority of the trainees are from the
unorganized sector.Priority should be given to candidates from roadside food outlets/dhabas/kiosks at tourist destinations etc. While
selecting candidates from rural areas preference would be given to artisans, craftsmen/women engaged in eco-tourism, etc. Application
ProcessOfflineFor the Trainee - Step 1: Eligible candidates can obtain application forms from the Institute office or from Institute's
Website. Step 2: The applicant should submit the duly filled application form along with all the documents and application fees. For
the Implementing Agency (Institute) - The Implementing agency will send the proposal in the prescribed format, along with the required
documents to the Ministry of Tourism.
