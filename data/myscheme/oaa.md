## Benefits

Eligible candidates in the 60–69 age
range financial assistance of Rs. 2,000/- per month. The government also gives SC/ST/Minority group members an extra Rs. 500/-
month.Rs. 2,500/- per month will be given to people over 70 years.

## Eligibility

Applicants must be 60 years or older.They must have
resided in Delhi for a minimum of five years before the date of application.The applicant's annual family income from all sources,
including rent, interest/dividends on savings and investments, earnings from farming, property sale proceeds, etc., should be less than Rs.
1,00,000.The applicant must have an Aadhaar number.The applicant must have a singly-operated account in any bank within the
National Capital Territory of Delhi only for receiving payment through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS).The applicant
must not be receiving any pension/financial assistance from the Central Government/State Government/other governmental local bodies
or any other source for this purpose.
