## Details

The “State
Rice Mission Scheme” is a State Plan Scheme, which is implemented by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare,
Government of Meghalaya. The objective of the scheme is to increase productivity and production of rice through improved technologies
and extension methods and, to supply the quality/certified seeds/organic fertilizers at 50% subsidy.

The applicant should be a permanent resident of Meghalaya.The applicant should be a
bonafide farmer engaged in agriculture.
